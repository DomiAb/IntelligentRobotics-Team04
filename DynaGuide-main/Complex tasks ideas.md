# Ideas of complex tasks to test

Remark: 

When the order is important, an idea could be to have a guidance having 100% of the first step, 75% of the second step, 62.5% of the third step, and so on. Thus, the first task may appear as a higher priority task. Success rate is still not expected to be 100%.

Otherwise, the main solution remains to have a different guidance for each step.

## Tasks with several independant actions

### move the red cube to the center + switch the light on

Expected result:
If the order is not important, expected to succeed.
If the order is important, cf remark above.

### close the drawer + lift the pink cube + open the door

Expected result:
Same as above, but maintaining the order may be even more difficult.

## Tasks with several dependant actions

### open the door and put the blue cube there

Expected result:
Expected to succeed.

### open the drawer, put the pink cube, then close the drawer

Expected result:
May have at least a tiny success rate using the remark above?

### open the drawer, put the red cube, open the door, put the blue cube, close the door, close the drawer

Expected result:
Same as above, with an even smaller success rate.
